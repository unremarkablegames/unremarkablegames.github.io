# CyberTank2K

December 31, 1999. The world is waiting to find out what happens at midnight.
You have a tank, a hostile arena, and a flag to bring to the exit. Make it there alive.

CyberTank2K combines pixel-art tank combat with a story that unfolds between rounds.
The briefings tell you what to do. What's really going on takes a little longer to uncover.

This is my first game with a narrative running through it. I started with a setting,
then added the manager. Now there are characters and a story to uncover.
There's a lot of work ahead.

**Estimated release: June 2027.**
